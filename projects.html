Can you beat a Markov chain?

An order-2 Markov chain that learns your rock paper scissors patterns in real time and plays the counter. Built to show that people are much worse at generating random sequences than they think.

Vanilla JavaScript, no dependencies.

Memory, stability and chaos in reservoir computing

Built an echo state network in Python and studied how the spectral radius drives the transition from stable to chaotic dynamics. Forecasted the Lorenz system with the reservoir and linked memory capacity to prediction accuracy, showing that forecasting peaks near the edge of chaos where the reservoir balances memory against stability.

Python, NumPy, SciPy.

Likelihood and Bayesian inference for COVID-19 vaccine efficacy

Estimated vaccine efficacy from clinical trial counts using a binomial likelihood model, deriving the maximum likelihood estimate and a confidence interval, then tested the efficacy hypothesis with a likelihood ratio test. Reframed the same problem in a Bayesian setting with a prior on the efficacy parameter and compared the frequentist and Bayesian conclusions.

R.
