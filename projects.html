Memory, Stability, and Chaos in Reservoir Computing

2026

Modeled an echo state network as a continuous-time dynamical system and derived where it stops being useful, showing the rest state destabilizes exactly when the spectral radius reaches the leakage rate. A numerical Lorenz benchmark then contradicted the popular claim that prediction works best at the edge of chaos, with accuracy peaking comfortably inside the stable regime instead.

Python, NumPy, SciPy, LaTeX

Generalizability of Sentiment-Based Stock Market Prediction

2026

A published study found plain logistic regression beating FinBERT and GPT-4 at predicting Nigerian market direction from news sentiment. I tested whether it transfers to the US, using eight years of Dow Jones closes paired with Reddit headlines. It does not. Accuracy collapsed from 82% to chance, and a likelihood ratio test found today's sentiment carries no signal at all. The model passes every diagnostic and still cannot tell an up day from a down day.

R, glmnet, TF-IDF, stepwise AIC

Likelihood and Bayesian Inference for COVID-19 Vaccine Efficacy

2026

Re-analyzed the Pfizer BNT162b2 trial data two ways. The likelihood approach put efficacy at 95.1%, matching the published result. A deliberately pessimistic Bayesian prior, one starting from the assumption that the vaccine does nothing, still produced a posterior median of 75.2% and placed the entire posterior above the FDA's 30% threshold. The point was to see whether the conclusion survives a skeptical starting position.

R, Bayesian statistics, Beta-binomial conjugacy, parametric bootstrap, likelihood ratio test
